STYLE DIRECTION - READ BEFORE CUTTING:

1. GLASSMORPHISM. Frame every screenshot/clip in a glassmorphism-style
   card, the frosted-glass look modern iPhone UI uses - a translucent
   panel with a soft blur behind it, a subtle light border, and a gentle
   shadow, rather than dropping the raw screenshot flat onto the
   background. Apply this consistently to every shot in the video, not
   just a few - it's a look for the whole piece, not an accent.

2. MOBILE <-> WEB TRANSITIONS. Every time a cut moves from a victim
   screenshot (mobile view, tall/narrow) to a staff/official screenshot
   (laptop/web view, wide) - or back the other way - use a real
   transition (a smooth cross-dissolve, a slide, or a device-frame morph
   that resizes one into the other) instead of a hard cut. This happens
   constantly and is the whole structure of BLOCK 3 (it alternates
   victim-mobile -> official-web five times in a row), so treat this as
   a consistent, deliberate transition style, not a one-off effect -
   a hard cut between two different aspect ratios and device chrome will
   read as a mistake, not a choice, if it isn't smoothed over every time.
